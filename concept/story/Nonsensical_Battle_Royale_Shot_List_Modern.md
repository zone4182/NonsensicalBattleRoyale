### GLOBAL VISUAL LANGUAGE

Every image below depicts the same fictional location, **Blackwood Manor**, as an established gothic Victorian country mansion. Maintain strong visual continuity between images: dark warm-gray stone, climbing ivy, steep slate roofs, ornate chimneys, tall arched windows, wrought-iron details, and an imposing but believable English country-estate architecture. The **architecture, furniture, and decor are entirely Victorian** — antique wood, carved detail, oil paintings, brass fixtures, candelabras.

However, the **story itself is set in the present day (21st century)**. This contrast is a deliberate visual motif: old-world gothic surroundings inhabited by modern life. Any people shown are **modern adults, styled as 40-something**, dressed in relaxed, informal, everyday clothing appropriate for a casual weekend getaway with friends — think jeans, sweaters, hoodies, sneakers, comfortable travel clothes — never period costume, never formalwear or business attire. **Modern technology exists and should appear where natural**: smartphones, tablets, laptops, modern cars in the driveway, modern wristwatches, contemporary luggage — placed in believable contrast against the antique backdrop (e.g., a glowing phone screen on a candlelit desk, a laptop closed on an antique writing table, a modern suitcase against a carved wooden chair).

Use **retro 16-bit / SNES-era pixel art with unusually high detail**, comparable to a modern pixel-art remaster: a visible pixel grid, painterly pixel clusters, rich color depth, soft gradient shading, atmospheric lighting, carefully rendered textures and cinematic composition. The image should feel like premium HD pixel-art game key art rather than crude low-resolution pixel art.

Landscape **3:2 aspect ratio, 1536×1024 composition**. Favor wide cinematic compositions, strong environmental storytelling and depth.

Humans, when present, must remain small or partially obscured, styled as modern 40-something adults in contemporary clothing. Do not render recognizable facial details. No gore-forward imagery. No UI, captions, speech bubbles, logos, watermarks. Physical in-world writing is allowed only when appropriate, and handwritten text should be indistinct rather than legible. Modern devices (phone/tablet/laptop screens) may appear but should show only soft glow or abstract light, never legible UI or text.

## NOTES ON CONSISTENCY

- **Architecture, furniture, and decor**: always Victorian gothic — never modernized.
- **People**: always styled as modern 40-something adults in relaxed, informal weekend clothing (jeans, sweaters, hoodies, sneakers) — never period costume, never formalwear or business attire — always small/obscured/faceless.
- **Technology**: smartphones, tablets, laptops, and a modern car are the recurring modern-day touches — introduced sparingly, as quiet contrasts rather than focal points, except where a scene's story beat calls for one to be prominent (e.g. the cracked phone in Shot 27, the abandoned phone in Shot 04).
- **The attic (Shot 22)** is intentionally the one space kept free of any modern object, reinforcing it as the mansion's oldest, most untouched layer.
